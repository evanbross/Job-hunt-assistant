In this app, I want a way to specify a list of company websites. I can give it the URLs to their jobs pages. Then in the background process, whenever this app is running, I wanted to scan those pages looking for new entries. so you'll have to keep the old ones around. Then take each one and weigh it against my profile just like is being done currently for jobs that came out of emails to see how good the fit is. Create a new table that lists the job, the website it came from, the confidence level, how good a match it is, and what the name of the job is, and a link to it.
- the job
- the website it came from
- the confidence level
- how good a match it is
 - the date the job was scanned
 - the date the job was posted on the site, if that's available.
- what the name of the job is
- a link to it
Then create a new tab in the top menu bar that lets me view the results from this background scanning. I want the interval at which things are scanned to be configurable in the .ENV file.
I also want buttons in a convenient location that will let me empty the contents of that table for these newly scanned things so I can start over and see what the testing is.
